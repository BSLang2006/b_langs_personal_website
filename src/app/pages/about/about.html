About

Hi — I'm Brandon.

I work in network operations. Most of my week is spent finding out why something stopped working and putting it back, which is a job I like more than I expected to.

I came to it late and sideways. Before this I drove and commanded light armored vehicles in the Marine Corps, rebuilt heavy-duty transmissions, and spent about a decade behind a bar — including the years Founders went from ten people to a hundred and fifty. Those are not the credentials anyone asks about, but they are where I learned the part of this job that is not technical: how to stay level when something is on fire, how to explain a problem to someone who does not want a lecture, and how to train the person coming up behind you.

The technical half came after — a cybersecurity degree, a programming degree, and three years of routers, switches, wireless, and VPNs across a hundred-odd sites. What I actually enjoy is the seam between the network and the software running on it. That is where the monitoring lives, and the automation, and the documentation, and it is usually the part nobody owns.

So I build things there. Most of what is on this site started as a problem in my own house that I could have solved by buying something, and didn't.
