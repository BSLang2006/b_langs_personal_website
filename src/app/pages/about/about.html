About

Brandon Lang

I work in network operations — isolating the Layer 2 and Layer 3 failures behind an outage, restoring service, and then writing down what actually happened so the next person does not start from zero.

Before this I was a team leader in the Marine Corps, which is where I learned that calm and specific beats fast and confident when something is broken and people are waiting. I have a degree in cybersecurity and another in computer programming, which means I tend to end up in the seam between the network and the software running on it — building the monitoring, the automation, and the documentation that neither side quite owns.

For the last three years I have deployed and supported routers, switches, wireless, and VPNs across residential and commercial sites, coordinating with ISPs and site engineers when a fault turned out to live upstream. Alongside it I run a lab at home that is genuinely useful rather than decorative — a self-hosted operations platform, LED fixtures I designed the firmware for, and enterprise routing scenarios built specifically so I can break them.
